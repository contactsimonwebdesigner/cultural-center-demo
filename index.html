WHO WE ARE

PROSTOR
FOR PEOPLE.

We're a nonprofit arts and community center that has brought people of all ages and backgrounds together for more than three decades.

We provide an open space for culture, learning, neighborhood gatherings, and community programs. We support local artists, initiatives, and everyone who wants to shape the place they call home.
